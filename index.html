How to play

  1. Deal every player one secret number from 1 to 10, all different. Cut ten slips of paper, or use a dice app.
  2. Draw a theme. It sets the scale: 1 is the low end, 10 is the high end.
  3. Each player names something that fits the theme at roughly their number. Never say a number, and never call your answer high or low outright.
  4. Compare, ask for more examples, then lay the slips face-up in ascending order, lowest first.
  5. Every slip played out of order costs a life. Three lives, three rounds.

Harder: two numbers each, or ban the first answer everyone thinks of.
