How to play (1–10 rules)
  1. Deal every player one secret number from 1 to 10 — all different. Cut ten slips of paper, or use a dice roll app.
  2. Draw a theme card above. It sets the scale: 1 is the low end, 10 is the high end.
  3. In turn, each player names something that fits the theme at roughly the value of their number. Never say a number and never say your thing is "high" or "low" outright.
  4. Talk, compare, ask for more examples. Then play your slips face-up in ascending order, lowest first.
  5. Every card played out of order costs the team a life. Three lives, three rounds — see how far you get.

Harder variant: two numbers each, or ban the first answer everyone thinks of.
